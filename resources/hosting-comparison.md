# Web Hosting Comparison — Fingerhut Law Group

**Domain:** fingerhutlawgroup.com  
**Current setup:** Hostinger (shared hosting + sfingerhut@fingerhutlawgroup.com)  
**Goal:** Professional, scalable hosting with multiple @fingerhutlawgroup.com email addresses

---

## Key Requirements

- Multiple professional email addresses (e.g., sfingerhut@, info@, intake@)
- Reliable uptime (clients must be able to reach the site)
- Scalable as the firm grows (staff, traffic, content)
- Professional look and feel — not a bargain-bin host
- Reasonable cost for a solo/small firm

---

## Option 1: Squarespace + Google Workspace (Recommended)

**Best for:** Polished, low-maintenance law firm site with easy content management

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Site hosting** | Squarespace Business plan — ~$23/mo (billed annually) |
| **Email** | Google Workspace Starter — $7/user/mo |
| **2 email addresses** | ~$37/mo total |
| **Domain** | Keep fingerhutlawgroup.com (transfer or point DNS) |

**Pros:**
- Squarespace templates are among the most professional-looking out of the box — no design skills needed
- Google Workspace gives you Gmail-powered @fingerhutlawgroup.com email, Google Calendar, Drive, Meet, and Docs
- Both platforms are fully managed — no server maintenance, no security patches
- Easy to add staff email addresses as the firm grows ($7/user/mo each)
- Strong uptime (99.9%+ SLA on both platforms)
- Built-in SSL, mobile optimization, and decent SEO tools

**Cons:**
- Less customizable than WordPress if you later want advanced features
- Squarespace is a closed ecosystem — migrating out means rebuilding the site
- Not the cheapest option

**Verdict:** Best balance of professional appearance, ease of use, and reliable email for a law firm that doesn't have an in-house developer.

---

## Option 2: WordPress (Self-hosted on SiteGround) + Google Workspace

**Best for:** Firms that want maximum control, SEO power, and room to grow

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Site hosting** | SiteGround GrowBig — ~$3.99/mo intro, ~$23/mo renewal |
| **Email** | Google Workspace Starter — $7/user/mo |
| **2 email addresses** | ~$37/mo ongoing |
| **Domain** | Keep fingerhutlawgroup.com |

**Pros:**
- WordPress powers 43% of the web — largest ecosystem of themes, plugins, and developers
- Best platform for long-term SEO (critical for attracting employment law clients)
- Full ownership of your content and code — never locked in
- SiteGround has excellent WordPress-specific support and automatic updates
- Scales easily: add pages, a blog, client intake forms, attorney bios, etc.
- Google Workspace email is the same as Option 1

**Cons:**
- Requires more setup and occasional maintenance vs. Squarespace
- Intro pricing nearly triples at renewal — budget for the renewal rate
- You are responsible for keeping plugins and themes updated (or pay for a managed plan)

**Verdict:** Best long-term choice if you plan to invest in SEO-driven content marketing (employment law blog posts, FAQ pages, etc.) to attract clients organically.

---

## Option 3: WordPress (Managed) on Kinsta + Google Workspace

**Best for:** High-performance, hands-off WordPress hosting as the firm scales

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Site hosting** | Kinsta Starter — $30/mo |
| **Email** | Google Workspace Starter — $7/user/mo |
| **2 email addresses** | ~$44/mo total |
| **Domain** | Keep fingerhutlawgroup.com |

**Pros:**
- Hosted on Google Cloud Platform — enterprise-grade speed and reliability
- Fully managed: daily backups, automatic updates, staging environment
- 24/7 expert WordPress support
- Excellent for a firm that grows to multiple attorneys with separate pages/profiles
- Free migrations from Hostinger

**Cons:**
- More expensive than SiteGround
- Overkill for a brand-new solo practice

**Verdict:** Worth considering once the firm has grown and the site is a revenue-critical asset. Premature at launch.

---

## Option 4: Microsoft 365 + Any Host

**Best for:** Firms already in the Microsoft ecosystem (Windows laptops, Outlook users)

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Email + Office** | Microsoft 365 Business Basic — $6/user/mo |
| **2 email addresses** | $12/mo for email alone |
| **Site hosting** | Pair with any host above |

**Pros:**
- Outlook desktop app is preferred by many attorneys for managing large email volumes
- Stronger compliance/eDiscovery tools (relevant for employment litigation)
- Full Office suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) included
- Lower per-seat cost than Google Workspace at entry level

**Cons:**
- Less intuitive than Gmail for quick mobile access
- Calendar/collaboration tools less seamless than Google
- You'd still need a separate hosting solution for the website

**Verdict:** Swap Google Workspace for Microsoft 365 in any of the options above if you prefer Outlook or are Windows-heavy. The price difference is minimal.

---

## Option 5: Stay on Hostinger (Not Recommended)

**Why to leave:**
- Hostinger is a budget host — shared infrastructure, inconsistent performance
- Support quality is limited compared to SiteGround, Squarespace, or Google
- Professional clients and opposing counsel will judge your digital presence; a slow or downtime-prone site reflects poorly
- Email reliability on Hostinger's own mail servers is lower than Google Workspace or M365
