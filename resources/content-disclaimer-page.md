## Page Body

### Firm Identity

This website is operated by **Fingerhut Law**, the registered trade name (assumed name) of **Fingerhut Law Group, PLLC**, a New York professional limited liability company. References on this website to "Fingerhut Law," "the firm," "Fingerhut Law Group," or "Fingerhut Law Group, PLLC" all refer to the same firm. Steven Fingerhut is the firm's founder and sole attorney.

### Attorney Advertising

This website is attorney advertising under the New York Rules of Professional Conduct. The content of this website is intended to provide general information about Fingerhut Law Group, PLLC and the legal services Steven Fingerhut provides to clients in New York and New Jersey. It is not intended as, and should not be relied upon as, legal advice for any specific situation.

### Prior Results

Prior results described on this website do not guarantee a similar outcome. Every legal matter is different. The outcome of any particular case depends on the specific facts, the applicable law, the venue, the parties, and many other variables that cannot be controlled or predicted by counsel. A successful verdict, settlement, or appellate result in a prior matter does not mean that a similar result will be achieved in any future matter, including yours.

### No Attorney-Client Relationship

Reviewing this website, contacting the firm through the website's contact form, sending email to any address shown on this website, or leaving a voicemail does **not** create an attorney-client relationship between you and Fingerhut Law Group, PLLC or Steven Fingerhut. An attorney-client relationship is formed only when (a) Steven Fingerhut has agreed in writing to represent you in a specific matter, (b) a written engagement or retainer agreement has been signed by both you and the firm, and (c) any conflict-of-interest review the firm conducts has been completed.

Until those steps occur, information you send to the firm — including details of your potential matter — may not be treated as confidential and may not be protected by the attorney-client privilege. Do not send the firm sensitive or confidential information about your potential matter until you have spoken with Steven Fingerhut and a representation has been confirmed in writing.

### Not Legal Advice

The information on this website, including descriptions of legal claims and statutes, summaries of New York and federal employment law, and any blog or insights content, is provided for general informational purposes only. It is not legal advice. Reading this website is not a substitute for consulting an attorney about your specific situation. Employment law is fact-intensive, statute-specific, and constantly evolving, and outcomes turn on details that cannot be addressed in a general-purpose article. If you believe your rights at work have been violated, you should consult an attorney about your particular circumstances.

### Jurisdictional Limits

Steven Fingerhut is admitted to practice in the State of New York and the State of New Jersey, and is admitted in the United States District Courts for the Southern, Eastern, Northern, and Western Districts of New York, the United States District Court for the District of New Jersey, and the United States Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit. He is not admitted to practice law in any other jurisdiction. Nothing on this website should be construed as an offer to provide legal services in any jurisdiction where Steven Fingerhut is not licensed.

### Communications Initiated by You

If you contact Fingerhut Law through the website, by telephone, or by email, you are doing so on your own initiative. The firm does not solicit individuals in a manner that violates the New York Rules of Professional Conduct. If at any time you wish to stop receiving communications from the firm, you may request that the firm cease contacting you and the firm will honor that request.

### Third-Party Links

This website may include links to third-party websites — including publishers of articles in which Steven Fingerhut has been featured, government agencies, and court systems. The firm does not control and is not responsible for the content, accuracy, or availability of third-party websites.

### Confidentiality of Form Submissions

Information you submit through the website's contact form is transmitted over a secure (HTTPS) connection and is reviewed by the firm. However, as noted above, contacting the firm through the form does not by itself create an attorney-client relationship, and information submitted through the form is not protected by the attorney-client privilege unless and until a representation has been confirmed in writing. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for additional detail on how the firm handles personal information submitted through the website.

## Notes for Steven before publishing

2. **Office address.** NY rules require firm websites to identify the firm's principal office address. Use a real address — this is one of the reasons GBP is also gated on the office lease, per `gbp_timing.md`.
3. **Bar admissions row.** The jurisdictional limits paragraph already lists the courts. Keep this in sync with the same list in the footer and in the bio (`content-bio-page.md`).
4. **Per-jurisdiction warnings.** If you ever publish content specific to a state in which you are not admitted (you currently are not, so skip this for now), you would need to add a per-page jurisdiction notice.
5. **Multi-state pages.** If the firm later opens a NJ office or adds NJ-specific landing pages, NJ has its own attorney advertising rules (RPC 7.1, 7.4) that must be reviewed.
