## Page Body

### Who This Policy Applies To

This Privacy Policy describes how Fingerhut Law Group, PLLC ("the firm," "we," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information from visitors to fingerhutlawgroup.com (the "site"). By using this site, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the site or submit information through it.

> *Editorial note: this is the only page on the site where "we" is unavoidable — it's industry-standard privacy-policy language. Everywhere else, the firm uses first-person singular per the firm-naming constraint.*

### What Information We Collect

**Information you provide.** When you submit the contact form on this site, you provide:
- Your name
- Your email address
- Your telephone number (if entered)
- The contents of any message you write, including any details about your situation that you choose to share

You may also email the firm directly at the addresses shown on the site, in which case the contents of your email and any information in your email signature are also received by the firm.

**Information collected automatically.** When you visit this site, the site's hosting platform (Squarespace) and the firm's analytics tools may automatically collect:
- Your IP address (and, derived from it, an approximate geographic location)
- The pages you view and the time you spend on each
- The website that referred you, if any
- Your browser type, device type, and operating system
- The date and time of your visit

This information is collected through cookies and similar technologies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; doing so may affect some features of the site but will not prevent you from contacting the firm by phone or email.

### How We Use the Information

The firm uses the information described above to:
- Respond to your inquiry and evaluate whether the firm can or should represent you
- Run a conflict-of-interest check before any consultation
- Improve the content and performance of the site
- Comply with legal and ethical obligations applicable to NY-licensed attorneys

The firm does not use information submitted through this site for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose unrelated to providing legal services or operating the site.

### Confidentiality

The firm treats inquiries with discretion, but please note: **information submitted through the website's contact form, or sent to the firm by email before an attorney-client relationship is formed, is not protected by the attorney-client privilege.** See the [Disclaimer & Attorney Advertising Notice](/disclaimer) for more detail. Do not send sensitive or confidential information about your potential matter until a representation has been confirmed in writing.

### Who We Share Information With

The firm does not sell or rent personal information. The firm does not share information submitted through this site with third parties for marketing purposes. The firm does share information with the following categories of service providers, only to the extent necessary to operate the firm and the site:

- **Hosting and form processing:** Squarespace (the platform that hosts the site and processes contact-form submissions). Squarespace's privacy practices are described at squarespace.com/privacy.
- **Email:** Google Workspace (Gmail), used by the firm for email. Google's privacy practices are described at policies.google.com/privacy.
- **Analytics:** the firm may use Squarespace Analytics and Google Analytics 4 to understand site usage. These tools collect the automatically-collected information described above.

The firm may also disclose information when required by law, court order, subpoena, or a binding ethical obligation (for example, a duty to report under the NY Rules of Professional Conduct).

### Data Retention

The firm retains contact-form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to evaluate the inquiry, respond to the sender, run a conflict check, and comply with the firm's professional record-keeping obligations. If you ask the firm to delete your inquiry, the firm will do so unless retention is required by law or by the firm's ethical obligations.

The firm retains client files separately and according to the New York Rules of Professional Conduct and the firm's file-retention policy (which is provided to clients at the start of a representation).

### Data Security

The firm uses reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, and disclosure, consistent with the requirements of New York's Stop Hacks and Improve Electronic Data Security ("SHIELD") Act. The site is served over HTTPS. Contact-form submissions are processed by Squarespace over encrypted connections. Email is hosted by Google Workspace and is protected by Google's security infrastructure.

No method of transmitting or storing information over the internet is perfectly secure, however. The firm cannot guarantee that information transmitted to or from the site will not be intercepted.

### Your Choices and Rights

You can:
- **Choose not to submit information** through the site
- **Disable cookies** in your browser
- **Ask the firm to delete** information you have submitted, by emailing sfingerhut@fingerhutlawgroup.com. The firm will respond within a reasonable time and will delete the information unless retention is required by law or by the firm's ethical obligations.
- **Opt out of Google Analytics** by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-On, available from Google

### Children

The site is intended for adults. The firm does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the site, please contact the firm and the information will be deleted.

### Out-of-State and International Visitors

This site is operated in and from the United States and is intended for residents of the United States — primarily, New York and New Jersey. If you access the site from outside the United States, you understand that any information you submit will be transferred to and processed in the United States and will be subject to U.S. law.

### Changes to This Policy

The firm may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of the policy will reflect any change. Material changes will be flagged prominently on the site.
