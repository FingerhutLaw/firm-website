## Bio (Main Body)

Steven Fingerhut is a New York employment law attorney who has recovered **over $30 million** in settlements and verdicts for workers who have been discriminated against, harassed, retaliated against, and wrongfully terminated.

After a decade at one of New York's largest plaintiffs-only employment law firms, Steven founded Fingerhut Law to provide workers with the kind of aggressive, personalized representation that levels the playing field against employers and their legal teams. He represents employees at Fortune 500 companies and small businesses alike — because workers at every level deserve a fearless advocate.

### Trial Record

Steven is an experienced trial attorney who takes cases to verdict.

In **January 2024**, he obtained a jury verdict in excess of **$425,000** in federal court after successfully trying hostile work environment, retaliation, and defamation claims on behalf of a client who was subjected to unlawful discrimination based on her sex and race and then terminated in retaliation for objecting to that harassment. *(Smart v. USA Labor for Hire, Inc., et al., Case No. 1:20-cv-5594 (E.D.N.Y.))*

In **May 2025**, Steven prevailed on each of the six grounds asserted by the defense on appeal, securing an affirmed judgment at the **Second Circuit Court of Appeals**. *(Case No. 24-1791)*

In **March 2022**, he obtained a unanimous jury verdict in Queens County Supreme Court for a client who was subjected to sexual harassment, assault, battery, and an unlawful constructive discharge — after being employed by the defendants for fewer than two weeks. The jury awarded **$112,000** in damages. *(Grant v. Pexie Enterprises, Inc., Index No. 704041/2015)*

He has also litigated and prevailed in one of **New York's first Paid Family Leave** cases, securing monetary compensation for a client who was wrongfully terminated from Vassar Brothers Medical Center while on approved leave caring for her infant son undergoing emergency surgery. *(In Regard to Stacey E. Johnson, PFL Case No. 000009 (May 2021))*
